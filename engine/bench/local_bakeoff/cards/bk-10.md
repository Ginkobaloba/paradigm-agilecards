# Fix parse_amount rejecting thousands separators

## Context

Imported price lists write amounts like `1,234.56`, and `parse_amount` raises `ValueError` on them. Commas must be accepted as thousands separators, but a misplaced comma must still be an error so that `1.234,5` (European style) is not silently misread.

## Scope

- In `tally/money.py`, let `parse_amount` accept commas in the whole-dollar part only when they are thousands separators: a leading group of 1 to 3 digits, then groups of exactly 3 digits, e.g. `1,234` or `$12,000` or `-1,000.5`.
- Any other comma placement raises `ValueError`, e.g. `1,23`, `,123`, `1,,234`, `1,234,`, `12,34.5`, `1.234,5`.
- Keep all existing behaviour for amounts without commas.
- The failing test is `tests/test_parse_amount.py`.

## Out of scope

- `format_cents` (another card owns it).
- Currency symbols other than `$`.
