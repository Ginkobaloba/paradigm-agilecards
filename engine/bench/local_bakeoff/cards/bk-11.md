# Add dates.add_business_days for delivery estimates

## Context

Delivery estimates are quoted in business days (Monday to Friday, no holiday calendar yet). We need a helper that moves a date forward by N business days.

## Scope

- Add `add_business_days(start: date, n: int) -> date` to `tally/dates.py`.
- Step forward one calendar day at a time and count only Monday to Friday; return the day on which the count reaches `n`. Example: Wednesday 2026-10-28 plus 3 is Monday 2026-11-02.
- `n == 0` returns `start` unchanged, even when `start` is a weekend.
- Negative `n` raises `ValueError`.
- The test is `tests/test_business_days.py`.

## Out of scope

- Public holidays.
- Changing `is_weekend` or `days_between`.
