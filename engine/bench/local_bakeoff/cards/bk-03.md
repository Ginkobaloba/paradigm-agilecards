# Rename Inventory.calc_total to total_value_cents

## Context

`Inventory.calc_total` does not say what it returns or in which unit. The team agreed to rename it to `total_value_cents` with no compatibility alias, since nothing outside this package calls it yet.

## Scope

- Rename the method `Inventory.calc_total` in `tally/inventory.py` to `total_value_cents`. Behaviour is unchanged.
- Update the caller in `tally/report.py` (`summary_line`).
- Do not keep an alias: remove every occurrence of the string `calc_total` from the `tally/` package, including docstrings and comments.
- The test is `tests/test_total_value.py`.

## Out of scope

- Changing how the total is computed.
- Other renames in the package.
